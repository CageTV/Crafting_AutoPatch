THE MODS THIS WORKS WITH

Crafting - AutoPatch contains no file from Weapons Armor Clothing and Clutter Fixes (Nexus 18994), Armor and Clothing Extension
(Nexus 19002) or Complete Crafting Overhaul Remastered (Nexus 28608), all by kryptopyr. It reads them while the game loads, adds
